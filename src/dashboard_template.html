From every delivery to two top-10 lists

The method answers one question: who performs best when the match is on the line? Each of the six steps removes one source of unfairness or noise, from the match situation to the quality of the opposition to the size of the sample. The figures on the right show how much data survives each step.

Which estimate predicts the future best?

We split matches randomly into two halves, 40 times. Each candidate measure was computed on one half and checked against pressure performance in the unseen half. Higher correlation means a better forecast.
